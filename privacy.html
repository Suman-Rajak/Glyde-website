Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026. This covers the Glyde app for Windows, Glyde Cloud (its server for Glyde AI), and this website.

In short. Glyde works on your PC. Your voice is turned into text on your PC, and what Glyde reads there (your clipboard, notifications, calendar, camera) stays there.

A few things go online, only when you use the feature that needs them, and each is listed below. Glyde has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell anything about you.

What stays on your PC

  • Your voice. When you talk to Glyde, what you say is turned into text on your PC by OpenAI's Whisper model, which Glyde downloads once from Hugging Face. The recording isn't kept. With "Hey Glyde" on, the microphone listens for those words on your PC, and nothing it hears is kept.
  • Your camera. The Prompter tab's mirror shows your camera live while you have it on. It's never recorded, kept or sent.
  • What you copy. The clipboard history is kept in memory. Only what you choose to save is written to your PC.
  • Notifications and calls from other apps, shown on the island. Glyde's activity log notes who they're from, never what they say.
  • Your calendar, read only, from the Windows calendar, for your next meeting.
  • What you ask Glyde to remember, your timers, alarms and reminders, files on the shelf, your teleprompter script, your settings and the activity log. All of it is in Glyde's own folder on your PC, and you can clear it from Settings.
  • Your coding agents' sessions (Claude Code and Codex). They're passed to Glyde on your own PC (127.0.0.1) and kept in memory only.

Glyde Cloud

Glyde AI runs through Glyde Cloud, our own server. It checks with the Microsoft Store that your account has Glyde Pro and Glyde AI, then passes your requests on to Anthropic, and gives the app short-lived passes for Deepgram and Microsoft. It keeps no record of what you ask. It does count how much each install uses each day, to keep everyone to a fair share. That count is kept under a random ID made by the app and a stand-in for your purchase, never your name or email.

Purchases

Glyde Pro and Glyde AI are bought through the Microsoft Store, which handles payment under Microsoft's own privacy statement. Your payment details never reach us.

What the app asks Windows for

Notifications, the microphone, the calendar, location and the camera. Windows asks you first, and Glyde's welcome explains why before it does. You can say no to any of them, or change them later in Windows Settings › Privacy & security.

This website

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, which form you used, and your time zone and language, in a Google Sheet only we can see. It's used to send you a welcome email and to tell you when Glyde launches. Emails are sent through Brevo or Gmail. Ask us and we'll delete your address.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics; the only thing it remembers, for that visit, is whether you closed its download bar.

Children

Glyde isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.
