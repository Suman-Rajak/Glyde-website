Every change worth knowing about, newest first.

Eyes, the thin line, and a tidier Settings

A new default character, a second way for the island to sit at the top, and Settings in eight clear tabs.

  • NewEyes: two simple eyes that blink, glance about and follow what’s happening. Glyde’s new default character.
  • NewThe island can now be a notch or a thin line over the top edge of the screen.
  • NewThe resting pill shows glances, like your next meeting, and a second bubble beside it.
  • BetterSettings is organised into eight tabs.
  • BetterThe music’s light now runs to the island’s edges.
  • BetterPoint & Ask reads the text on your screen, so its answers are more exact.
  • FixedGlyde always finishes quitting when you close it.
  • SoonGlyde AI and Glyde AI Max, by the month, with a lower price if you own Glyde Pro.

Help in your browser

A new look for the assistant

Glyde now shows what it’s doing at a glance, and lights the island from below while it listens, thinks and talks.

  • NewAn orb shows the assistant at work: a dot while it listens, a turning ring while it thinks, bars while it talks.
  • NewEight natural voices that run on your PC. Pick one from tiles with pixel faces.
  • NewThe music pill shows the lyrics, and the island’s light moves with the beat.
  • NewPoint & Ask works sums out on a handwritten board beside your screen.
  • BetterPoint & Ask draws its circles, arrows and labels more precisely.
  • BetterEsc stops the assistant, and the chimes are softer.
