Private by design.

An app that sees your music, messages and clipboard has to earn that. Here's how Glyde does.

Your voice stays home

Speech is turned into text on your PC. Recordings are never kept, logged or sent.

Clipboard stays put

Kept in memory and gone when Glyde quits, except what you bookmark to keep, which stays on your PC. Never synced.

Passwords skipped

Anything your password manager marks as private never enters the history, using the same markers as Windows.

Online only when it must

Cover and lyrics lookups send just the song. Glyde AI sends only what your PC can't answer, and Glyde's servers keep no record of it. Settings lists every one, and each can be switched off.

Questions, answered.

When can I get Glyde?

It's in the final stretch. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email the day it's out, with the early-bird price on Pro.

Should I download it here or from the Microsoft Store?

Glyde Pro and Glyde AI are bought in the Microsoft Store and kept with your Microsoft account, so get Glyde from the Store if you plan to buy them. It installs in a click and keeps itself up to date. There's no account to make: purchases use the Microsoft account you're already signed in with, which is also how they unlock on your other PCs.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Everything in Glyde Pro is unlocked for the first 14 days. After that, Glyde stays free: the island, music and lyrics, the notices, live scores and the clock are yours for good.

Which versions of Windows does it run on?

Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) and Windows 11.

Which music apps work?

Any app that tells Windows what it's playing: Spotify, YouTube in Chrome or Edge, Amazon Music, Media Player and many more. When an app doesn't share artwork, Glyde can look it up (or you can switch that off).

Is Glyde always listening?

No. By default Glyde only listens after you press the shortcut or tap the mic. “Hey Glyde” is opt-in, and even then only the first two seconds after a pause are checked for the name, on your PC.

What's Glyde AI, and what does it cost?

It's coming soon, in two sizes. Glyde AI is ₹299 a month (₹199 if you own Glyde Pro), and Glyde AI Max is ₹1,499 a month (₹1,349 with Pro). It answers anything (with a picture when it helps), writes emails and WhatsApps, works on what you've copied, and gives Glyde a natural voice and instant hearing. There are no keys to set up, and you can cancel any time from your Microsoft account. Without it, every command on your PC still works.

What are coding agents?

If you code with an AI coding tool in the terminal, Glyde shows each session on the island: what it's doing, when it's done, and when it needs you. Allow or Deny its requests right there, and get a soft ping when one is waiting while you're in another window. Before it does something that can't be undone, like wiping a database or deleting a folder, it waits: the island glows red, Glyde says why, and only the island can allow it. They come with Glyde Pro.

Will it get in the way of games or films?

No. It hides itself whenever a fullscreen app, game or video is in front (YouTube in your browser included), clicks pass straight through everywhere except the pill, and clicking the pill never steals focus. Glyde's speech model is only loaded while you use it and unloads after ten minutes.

I have more than one monitor.

Pick which display the island sits on in Settings, or let it follow your pointer from screen to screen.
